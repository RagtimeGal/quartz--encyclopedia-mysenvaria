Besides the [[_index|Encyclopedia Mysenvaria]], [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Organizations/Academy Publishing|Academy Publishing]] also offers an extensive catalogue of archived [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Arts/Art#Literature|literary works]], many of which our scholars have worked diligently to translate. Whilst the archival library is available to all guests of the Academy it is primarily intended to aid our scholars in their continuous mission to build the Encyclopedia. For that reason only a small portion of the archive is available to guests, primarily consisting of newer and more well-maintained works. Guests are permitted to request works which may not be publicly accessible from attending archivists.
