Elven languages are the family of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Technologies/Language|languages]] invented by and used by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organisms/Plants/Elven Fungus|elves]]. Elves lack of the ability to speak means that elven languages, bar various constructed languages, are not spoken and are instead signed and written. Elven languages are divided into [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Northern Continent|New World]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Southern Continent|Old World]] language groups.

[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Southern Continent|Old World]] elves who practice various sciences in close proximity with dwarves and humans have adapted the use of artificially created respiratory systems—including fake lungs, trachea, and larynx—to replicate human and dwarven speech. This has given rise to a small number of trade languages spoken exclusively by these speaking elves and their colleagues.
