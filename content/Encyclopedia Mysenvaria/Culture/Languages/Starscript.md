Starscript is hypothesized to be a result of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Natural Personification|Natural Personification]], specifically Relational Concentration, and is a language which exists only in the written form of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|stars]]. Starscript is believed to be a primordial language which has existed since the creation of Reality, as modelled by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Universal Strata Model|Universal Strata Model]]. Starscript is well studied by [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Occupations & Crafts/Astronomer|astronomers]] but not well understood, and it is unclear if the language can ever truly be understood in its entirety.

Starscript does not exist in a spoken form, as confirmed by multiple [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]]. Rather, it exists only in a written form, as true stars. All that is truly understood of Starscript is that it models the true relationship between the Real and Unreal and is capable of modifying that relationship.
