The afterlife is a speculative existence after death in which individuals consciousness and identities continue to exist after the death of their physical body. Afterlives are often, but not always, believed to be overseen by [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Beliefs/Deities|deities]]. The first mention of the afterlife can be traced back to the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Afterlife System/Afterlife Star|Afterlife Star]], published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Energy Gods/God of Chromodynamic Energy|God of Chromodynamic Energy]] shortly following the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Convening|First Convening]] on the 3rd of Summer, [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/180s BT#176 BT|176 BT]]. The Afterlife Star documented Chromodynamic Energy's various ideas and concepts surrounding the afterlife and its possible existence. The star made no mention of deities, an idea which would not develop for another hundred years until the creation of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organisms/Animals/Human|humans]] in [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/60s BT#56 BT|56 BT]].
