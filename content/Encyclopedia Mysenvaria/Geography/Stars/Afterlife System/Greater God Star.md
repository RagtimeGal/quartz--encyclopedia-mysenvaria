The Greater God Star is a [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|star]] in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Afterlife System|Afterlife System]] which orbits the system host, the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Afterlife System/Afterlife Star|Afterlife Star]]. Published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Compound Gods/God of Ethionamide|God of Ethionamide]] the star is the origin of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Beliefs/Greater God Conspiracy|Greater God Conspiracy]], purporting that, though the reason is unclear, the actions of the higher-[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]] are advancing an agenda against the lesser-Gods.
