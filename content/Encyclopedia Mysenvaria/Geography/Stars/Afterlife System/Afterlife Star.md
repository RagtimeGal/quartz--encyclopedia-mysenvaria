The Afterlife Star is the host [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|star]] of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Afterlife System|Afterlife System]]. Published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Energy Gods/God of Chromodynamic Energy|God of Chromodynamic Energy]] the star documents many of the ideas and concepts surrounding the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Beliefs/Afterlife|afterlife]] which Chromodynamic Energy theorized about during the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Convening|First Convening]]. In the star Chromodynamic Energy would draw the conclusion that there must be an [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Beliefs/Afterlife|afterlife]]. While the star would garner its own system soon after its publication it would see a much larger expansion of its system following the creation of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organisms/Animals/Human|humans]].
