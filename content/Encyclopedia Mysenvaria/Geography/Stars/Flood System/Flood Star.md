The Flood Star is the host [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|star]] of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Flood System|Flood System]]. Published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Compound Gods/God of Water|God of Water]], the star served as a signal to other [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]] and some [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Organisms|Organisms]] to begin with their part in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Great Flood|Great Flood]].
