The Flood System is a major [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|star]] system created in the year 25 BT, immediately preceding the beginning of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Great Flood|Great Flood]] and and in the midst of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Water Wars|Water Wars]]. The host star, the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Flood System/Flood Star|Flood Star]], published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Compound Gods/God of Water|God of Water]], was a signal for various other [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Organisms|Organisms]] to begin with their part in the Great Flood.
