The Quickening Star is the host star of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Quickening Star System|Quickening Star System]]. The star was created by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Elemental Gods/God of Carbon|God of Carbon]] to document their creation of many of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Biographies/Gods/List of Compound Gods|carbon compound Gods]].
