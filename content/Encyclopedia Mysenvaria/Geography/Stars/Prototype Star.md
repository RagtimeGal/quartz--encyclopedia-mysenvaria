The Showcase Star is a star published in 55bt, published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Compound Gods/God of Hydrogen Cyanide|God of Hydrogen Cyanide]]. The star details their use of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Species/Human|humans]] as an assassination tool, and served as a kind of pitch to other [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]] to convince them to use humans.
