The Cilia Star is a [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|star]] created by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Compound Gods/God of Aspartic Acid|God of Aspartic Acid]]. The star documents their creation of their own [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Taxa/Animal|animals]]. The Cilia Star is related to, but not a direct descendant of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Mitosis Star System|Mitosis Star System]].
