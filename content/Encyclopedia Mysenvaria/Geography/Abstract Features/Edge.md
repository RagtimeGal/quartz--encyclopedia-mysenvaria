The Edge is an abstract geographic feature equidistant in all directions from the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Abstract Features/Central Pole|Central Pole]] which represents the furthest part of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Plane|Plane's]] [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Habitable Zone|habitable zone]]. The Edge is where the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Atmosphere|atmosphere]], [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Abstract Features/Heavens|heavens]], and Plane all converge and where the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Great Fog|Great Fog]] begins. The Edge is not only an important [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Geography|geographic]] reference point, but also an important scientific curiosity that explains a lot about the natural world.[^figure1]
# Geography
Roughly 60% of the Edge falls on land, the other remaining area occupying the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Ocean|ocean]]. The entirety of the Edge is bitterly cold, a result of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Oceanic Circulation|oceanic]] and [[Meta/Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Atmospheric Circulation|atmospheric circulation]]. Winds and water which reach the Edge have simply lost too much energy on their journey from the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Abstract Features/Harmony Line|harmony line]] to significantly warm the air. For this reason, much of the Edge is covered in [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Ice Sheets|ice sheets]], some of which are seasonal, but most of which are year-round. 

While there is no solid barrier stopping passage through the Edge the Great Fog does appear as a giant living wall hugging the atmosphere. It is impossible for life which requires oxygen to survive passing through the Edge and into the Fog without suffocating. It is also impossible for [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]] to pass into the Fog as is decreed by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/First Epoch Star|First Epoch Star]] which created the Plane and Fog.
# History
During the start of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era|God-War]] the Plane had an extent significantly larger than it is today. Stars like [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Border Operation|Border Operation]], [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Frontier Beyond|Frontier Beyond]], and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Claim Star|Claim Star]] detail the lives and territories of lesser-[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]] who lived beyond the modern Edge. Following the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God Councils#First Council|First God Council]] the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Fog Star|Fog Star]] would be published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|Creation Quadrinity]] with the help of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of What Is and Is Not|What Is and Is Not]] and the confines of the world would shrink to resemble the modern day's Edge. The shrinking of the Edge would lead to an event known as the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/The Great Poleward Fog Migration|The Great Poleward Fog Migration]], wherein millions of Gods would race against the Great Fog so as to not be consumed.

Throughout the rest of the Early-War Epoch the Edge would become a somewhat favorable place to hold [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Territories During the God-War|territory]] as it removed an entire direction that needed to be defended. However, the onset of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Organisms|organisms]] in the Mid-War Epoch would see these territories become less favorable as life could not as easily survive the frigid temperatures. Gods would employ a number of tools in an attempt to subvert life's inoperability at these temperatures, though many of these methods would not survive to the end of the war.
