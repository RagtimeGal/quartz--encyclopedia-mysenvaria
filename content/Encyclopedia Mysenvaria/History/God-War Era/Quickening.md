The Quickening is one of the most influential events in [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/History|history]]. Having occurred over the span of 58 days—less than a single season—it saw the creation of every known and unknown God, and was the root cause of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Death|First Death]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era|God-War]]. The Quickening is documented in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Quickening Star System|Quickening Star System]], composed of 430 [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|stars]] published by various [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]] documenting their own domain subdivisions.

The number of Gods crated during the Quickening i a highly debated topic by historians. The most agreed upon estimates place the number of Gods created somewhere in the range of 25 to 30 million. Though some historians argue the number could be as low as 5 million to as high as the low billions. Most of these estimates are made based on [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Death's Star System/Death's Star|Death's Star]], which is often translated as recording the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Dying Period|Dying Period's]] death toll as somewhere between 90-99% of all Gods.
# Prelude & Aftermath
Prior to the Quickening, on the 12th of Winter [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/180s BT#178 BT|178 BT]], documented in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/First Epoch Star|First Epoch Star]], the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of What Is and Is Not|Gods of What Is and Is Not]] underwent the first instance of domain subdivision, creating the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space]]. 46 days after their creation, documented in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Quickening Star System/First Lessers Star|First Lessers Star]], having used the First Epoch Star as reference, The God of Matter would undergo their own subdivision to create the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Elemental Gods|Elemental Gods]]. This action would inspire the God of Energy to undergo their own subdivision, documented in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Quickening Star System/Energy Division Star|Energy Division Star]], creating the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Energy Gods|Energy Gods]]. This subdivision process would quickly cascade down the God's lineage, resulting in the creation of an estimated fifteen billion Gods.

The gargantuan number of Gods would peak on the 26th of Spring [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/180s BT#177 BT|177 BT]], where the rising tensions between Gods would culminate in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Death|First Death]]. [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Death's Star System/Death's Star|Death's Star]], published by the God of What Is Not, attempted to document the confrontation, which occurred between two unnamed lesser-Gods. In the following days, as Gods became increasingly aware of their mortality, Death's Star would receive an influx of stars, creating [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Death's Star System|Death's Star System]].
