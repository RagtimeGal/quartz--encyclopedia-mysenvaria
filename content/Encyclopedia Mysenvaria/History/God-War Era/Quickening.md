The Quickening—an event which occurred over the span of less than a [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Seasons|season]]—is widely considered to be one of the most influential in [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/History|history]], being the root cause of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Death|First Death]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era|God-War]]. The event is documented in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Quickening Star System|Quickening Star System]], composed of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|stars]] published by various [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]] documenting their own domain subdivisions. The host star, the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Quickening Star System/Quickening Star|Quickening Star]], was published by the God of Matter to document their creation of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Biographies/Gods/List of Elemental Gods|Elemental Gods]].
# Prelude & Aftermath
Before the start of the Quickening the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of What Is and Is Not|Gods of What Is and Is Not]] underwent the first instance of domain subdivision, documented in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/First Epoch Star|First Epoch Star]], the duo created the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space]]. This first division by What Is and Is Not would inspire the God of Matter to divide their territory amongst the Elemental Gods. This division by Matter would in turn inspire the God of Energy to also undergo subdivision. This process would continue to recur, cascading down as Gods subdivided, resulting in the creation of an estimated fifteen billion Gods.

The astronomical number of Gods would lead to overlapping domains and cramped space. Documented in [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Death's Star System/Death's Star|Death's Star]] the increasing tensions would culminate in a confrontation between two lesser-Gods, resulting in the First Death in 177 BT. Gods, now aware of their own mortality, would begin panicking and the God-War would begin.
