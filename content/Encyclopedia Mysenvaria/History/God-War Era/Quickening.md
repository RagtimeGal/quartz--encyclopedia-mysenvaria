The Quickening—an event which occurred over the span of less than a [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Seasons|season]] in the year [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/180s BT#178 BT|178 BT]]—is widely considered to be one of the most influential in [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/History|history]], being the root cause of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Death|First Death]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era|God-War]]. The event is documented in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Quickening Star System|Quickening Star System]], composed of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|stars]] published by various [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]] documenting their own [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Natural Subdivision|natural subdivisions]]. The host star, the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Bernie's Star System/Quickening Star|Quickening Star]], was published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|God of Matter]] to document their creation of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Biographies/Gods/List of Elemental Gods|Elemental Gods]].
# Prelude & Aftermath
Before the start of the Quickening the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Life and Death|Gods of Life and Death]] underwent the first instance of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Natural Subdivision|natural subdivision]], documented in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/First Epoch Star|First Epoch Star]], the duo created the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space]]. This first division by Life and Death would inspire the God of Matter to divide their territory amongst the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Biographies/Gods/List of Elemental Gods|Elemental Gods]]. This division by Matter would in turn inspire the God of Energy to also undergo subdivision. This process would continue to recur, cascading down as Gods subdivided, resulting in the creation of an estimated fifteen billion Gods.

The astronomical number of Gods would lead to overlapping domains and cramped space. Documented in [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Death's Star System/Death's Star|Death's Star]] the increasing tensions would culminate in a confrontation between two [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Perennial Classification|Lesser-]]Gods, resulting in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Death|First Death]] in 177 BT. Gods, now aware of their own mortality, would begin panicking and the God-War would begin.
