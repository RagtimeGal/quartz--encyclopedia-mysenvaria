One of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era|God-Wars]] most defining historical traits was the ever-changing landscape, not just [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Geography During the God-War|topographically]] but politically. The territories of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]] did not just exist in [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Universal Strata#Layers|reality]] but also across unreality, though it is easier to quantify and understand the physical disputes which are documented in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|stars]]. [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Occupations & Crafts/Historian|Historians]] seek to understand the way Gods territories changed during the war to better understand how the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Plane|Plane]] came to be the way it is known today.
