The 180s BT was a decade full of rising tension, marking the beginning of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era|God-War Era]] in 177 BT.
# Events
## 178 BT
- The [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Life and Death|Gods of Life and Death]] publish the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/First Epoch Star|First Epoch Star]], the very first [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|star]].
- The host star of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Bernie's Star System|Bernie's Star System]], [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Bernie's Star System/Bernie's Star|Bernie's Star]], is published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|God of Matter]] documenting their creation of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Biographies/Gods/List of Elemental Gods|elemental Gods]] through [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Natural Subdivision|natural subdivision]]. This is the beginning of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Quickening|Quickening]].
## 177 BT
- The death of an unknown God marks the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Death of a God|first time a God has ever died]]. This event marks the beginning of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era|God-War Era]].
- The host star of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Death's Star System|Death's Star System]], [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Death's Star System/Death's Star|Death's Star]], is published by an unknown God, but republished by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Life and Death|God of Death]].
## 176 BT
- [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/The First Convening|The First Convening]] is held by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Phenomena Gods/Gods of Earth/God of Fulgurite|God of Fulgurite]], gathering together 16 Gods who had witnessed the first death. Attendees included such names as the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Energy Gods/God of Chromodynamic Energy|God of Chromodynamic Energy]], [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Compound Gods/God of Sodium Ethyl Xanthate|Sodium Ethyl Xanthate]], [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Compound Gods/God of Hydrogen Cyanide|Hydrogen Cyanide]], [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Compound Gods/God of Water|Water]], [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Phenomena Gods/Gods of Earth/God of Tectonics|Tectonics]], and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Phenomena Gods/Gods of Weather/God of Weather|Weather]]. Much of what was discussed is published in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Convening Star|Convening Star]], originally published by Fulgurite, later republished by Sodium Ethyl Xanthate.
- The [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Energy Gods/God of Chromodynamic Energy|God of Chromodynamic Energy]] publishes the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Afterlife System/Afterlife Star|Afterlife Star]], concluding that the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Beliefs/Afterlife|afterlife]] must exist based off of discussion from the First Convening.
## 175 BT
- [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Spatial Act|Spatial Act]] is forcibly passed by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|Gods of Time and Space]].
