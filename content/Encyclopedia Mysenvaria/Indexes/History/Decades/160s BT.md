# Events
## 160 BT
- 87th of Autumn — The [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Dying Period|Dying Period]] ends (beg. 177 BT) after the passing of three quarters of all [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]] which have ever existed. An estimated 11.2 billion Gods have passed away in seventeen years, over 1.9 million Gods per day.
