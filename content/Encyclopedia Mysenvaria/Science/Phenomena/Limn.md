Limn is a product of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Natural Personification|Natural Personification]], as modelled in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Universal Strata Model|Universal Strata Model]] it is a product of other parts of Nature creeping into Reality. Limn is a strange Real-esque [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Entities|entity]] which does not obey the laws of the Unreal. Limn is not composed of typical [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Material Structure|material structure]], but instead appears to consist of a grid-like fractal pattern which repeats infinitely.

Limn subverts measurement, it is weightless but takes on a defined form and volume. Limn is easily compressed into larger segments by binding its fractal structures together, and easily torn apart by shearing these structures apart.
# Production & Application
Limn naturally materializes throughout the whole of Reality, appearing at varying microscopic sizes. Limn is accumulated in ecosystems through the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Food Chain|food chain]], absorbed passively by [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Organisms|organisms]] like [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Taxa/Plant|plants]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Taxa/Vermin|vermin]]. These basal organisms do not typically use the Limn for anything in particular, it is not until further up in the food chain that organisms use special [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organs/Limnary Press|limnary press]] organs to turn digested Limn into useful [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars#Artificial Stars|artificial stars]].

Limn is used by [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Species/Human|humans]], [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Species/Dwarf|dwarves]], and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Disease/Elf|elves]] to craft their own artificial stars, though in all three of these organisms there is no such limnary press present. Artificial stars themselves are used for a variety of purposes, but for more information on their use see the page on [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|stars]].

Limn is harvested by different cultures across the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Plane|Plane]] in varying ways.
