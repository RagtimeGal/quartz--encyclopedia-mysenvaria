Gravity is a fundamental force of Reality defined in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/First Epoch Star|First Epoch Star]]. It is the reason for many other phenomena across the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Plane|Plane]], such as [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Tectonics|tectonics]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Weather|weather]]. Gravity is the reason the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Harmonic Treaty|Harmonic Treaty]] impacts the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Atmosphere|atmosphere]] and creates [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Atmospheric Circulation|atmospheric circulation]]. It is the reason [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|stars]] fall back to the Plane when their publishing [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|God]] dies.
# History
[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Sophonts|Sophonts]] have conceptualized and acknowledged the existence of gravity since the creation of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organisms/Animals/Human|humans]] by [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Phenomena Gods/Gods of Earth/God of Porous Rock|Porous Rock]]. Early God-backed city-states during the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era|God-War Era]] saw the proliferation and spread of knowledge throughout society, and especially the trade of information between Gods and sophonts. Surviving artifacts from the God-War show humanity had known about and understood gravities existence and strength even during societies infancy.
