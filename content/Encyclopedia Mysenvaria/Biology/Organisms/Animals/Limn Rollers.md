Limn Rollers are small beetles which collect [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Limn|limn]], primarily from excrement, and roll it into [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|artificial stars]] which are commonly used to cast elaborate display spells.
