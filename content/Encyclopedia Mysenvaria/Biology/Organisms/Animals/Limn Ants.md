Limn Ants are a small group of ants made up of species which collect limn. Species range from across the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Northern Continent|New]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Southern Continent|Old World]]. Certain species of limn ant have been domesticated and are used by [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Occupations & Crafts/Mage|mages]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Occupations & Crafts/Wizard|wizards]] for the procurement of limn.
