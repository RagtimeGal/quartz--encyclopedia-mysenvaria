Logbugs are a small group of large insectoids which mimic various species of tree. They are often accompanied closely by leafbugs, though it is unclear if the two share any relation outside of symbiosis. While a few species are found in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Southern Continent|Old World]] they appear to be more diverse and have larger ranges in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Northern Continent|New World]].
