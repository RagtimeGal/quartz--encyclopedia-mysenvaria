Humans are a [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Sophonts|sophont]] species of omnivorous disruptive bipeds. Humans were invented by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Phenomena Gods/Gods of Earth/God of Porous Rock|God of Porous Rock]] in the year [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/60s BT#56 BT|56 BT]]. Whilst Porous Rock themself would not publish a star documenting the creation of humans their relative, [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Phenomena Gods/Gods of Earth/God of Igneous Rock|Igneous Rock]] would publish [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Alex's Star System/Alex's Star|Alex's Star]], a furious rebuttal against the creation of humanity. The creation of humans would be directly responsible for the holding of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God Councils#Second Council|Second God Council]] and the passing of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Creations Act|Creations Act]].

Humans have varying melanistic patterns across their bodies which serve as markers of their creator [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|God]] and ethnic background, these patterns may be passed down genetically. Their skin without melanin is slightly translucent and colored, in a way best described as resembling opal.
