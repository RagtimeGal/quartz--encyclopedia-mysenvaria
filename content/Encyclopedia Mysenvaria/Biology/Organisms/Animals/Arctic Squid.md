Arctic Squid area a species of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organisms/Animals/Terrestrial Squid|Terrestrial Squid]] native to the polar regions of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Habitable Zone|habitable Plane]]. They are found in both the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Northern Continent|New]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Southern Continent|Old World]]. Their bodies are completely white to blend into the snow and ice of these polar regions.
