Mimic Owls are a species of extremely large owl native to the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Northern Continent|New World]] which are capable of mimicking other animals profiles in the dark. By raising or lowering specific feathers across their bodies and extending their wings in certain ways mimic owls are capable of making their profiles in the dark look like the profiles of their prey. They are also capable of nearly completely silent movement and mimicking the sounds and mannerisms of their prey so as to not raise attention in the dark.
