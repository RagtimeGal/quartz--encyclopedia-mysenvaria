Mimic Cuttlefish are a species of extremely large and terrestrial cuttlefish. They get their name from their impeccable mimicry, which allows them to shape their bodies into practically any organism of roughly similar or smaller size than themselves. They use this mimicry for hunting. They are found exclusively in tropical regions of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Northern Continent|New World]].
