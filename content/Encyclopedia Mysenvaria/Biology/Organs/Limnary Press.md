Limnary presses are [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Organ|organs]] possessed by [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Organisms|organisms]] which are capable of producing [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|artificial stars]]. They are part of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Organ Systems#Limnary System|limnary system]] of the body. In organisms which possess them, limn which would normally make its way into excrement is recycled and used to produce stars so that spells may be used. Only a select few organisms, most notably [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Sophonts|sophonts]], but also organisms like [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organisms/Animals/Limn Rollers|limn rollers]], are capable of producing artificial stars without the use of a limnary press.
