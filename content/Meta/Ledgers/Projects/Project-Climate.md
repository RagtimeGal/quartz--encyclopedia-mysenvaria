> [!summary] Project: Climate
> **Started**: 46/2024
> **Deadline:** 48/2024
> **Deadline Reached:** WIP

*Climate*'s objective is to complete Mysenvar's climate and have it in a usable state by the 48th week of this year. This includes fleshing out scientific models, scientific phenomena, and geographical features directly related to the climatology of the Plane. This project's scope is small but has a large impact on various other sections of the Encyclopedia.

Phenomena like atmospheric cells, oceanic circulation, and tectonic plates should be developed. Scientific models for climate classification systems, atmospheric circulation, and tectonics should be developed. Related pages to these topics should also be developed, including primarily various geographical features, scientific technologies, and scientific branches.

Completing this objective should begin from the ground up, working to create visual elements to aid writing across the pages which develop the climatology.
