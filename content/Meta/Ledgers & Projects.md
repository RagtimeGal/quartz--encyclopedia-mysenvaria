> *This article is part of a series of meta articles explaining what the Encyclopedia Mysenvaria is, how to use it, and how to contribute.*

The Encyclopedia Mysenvaria is built using [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) and hosted on the web using [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). Because of the way the encyclopedia is constructed, it updates automatically when new content is pushed to the git. This makes maintaining the encyclopedia extremely easy and hassle-free. From the projects GitHub you can view all pushes and merges, effectively serving as a list of the encyclopedias updates.

To the average reader who isn't a robot and isn't well-versed in Git this probably isnt that useful. So, to ensure a reader-focus and maintain accessibility , Lore Ledgers are maintained to document changes across the encyclopedia and progress across ongoing Projects. Lore Ledgers are reader-focused and serve not just as changelogs but also progress trackers of ongoing Projects across the encyclopedia.


Lore Ledgers are articles used to document changes across the encyclopedia, think of them like a changelog. Ledger changelogs will generally look something like this:
